Assignment 3: The Flickering Pink (and Green) Torus World
• Hold SHIFT + Click & Drag to place and scale a torus.
• Dragging down flips through scale 0 to turn grassgreen.
• Dragging back up flips to turn hotpink.
• Release mouse to resume camera orbit / zoom.
• Press F to toggle random flickering.
• Press W to toggle wireframe mode.
